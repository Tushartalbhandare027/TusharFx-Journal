Features

Built to change behaviour, not just record trades

Most journals tell you what happened. This one flags rule breaks as they happen and shows which habits cost you money.

!

Risk rules & alerts

Max trades per day, daily and weekly loss limits and lot-size checks. Breaches are flagged on every trade and counted on the dashboard.

☼

Sessions & liquidity sweeps

Tag Asia, London, New York or the overlap, plus the sweep type. Compare results with and without a sweep.

✓

7 confirmations & grading

Structure shift, displacement, FVG, rejection, HTF alignment, killzone and news. The count becomes an A+ to C grade.

⏱

Hour & hold-time analysis

Find your best and worst entry hours and see whether you hold losers longer than winners.

☰

Daily journal

Weekday rows with bias, key levels, sleep, mood, focus and an end-of-day review. Analytics links mood and sleep to results.

★

Playbook

Write the rules for each setup and get a live Keep / Refine / Review verdict from your results.

⚑

Prop-firm tracker

Progress bars for profit target, daily loss and drawdown against your challenge limits.

⇆

Planned vs actual

Planned entry and stop against what you did: entry slippage in pips and how often the stop moved.

≡

Lot size & pips

Pip results and a suggested lot size from your risk, stop distance and pip value, so you can match your broker statement.

◎

Goals

Monthly targets for R, win rate, adherence and return, shown next to your actual results.

⚒

Backtest log

Log tested trades in R to validate a setup before risking money, with a live-versus-backtest gap by setup.

☷

Multiple accounts

Live, demo and prop accounts each keep their own balance and drawdown. Switch the dashboard view with one dropdown.

Ready in five minutes

Open the demo first to see a filled-in journal, then switch to the blank file.

  1. DownloadGet the blank journal and, if you like, the demo file to see it populated.

Good to know

Questions

Which spreadsheet apps work?

It is built for Microsoft Excel 2019 or Microsoft 365 and has been recalculated in LibreOffice. Some formulas use MAXIFS and MINIFS, so older Excel versions may not calculate everything. It has not been tested in Google Sheets.

Are the balances and prop limits real?

No. The account balances (10,000 and 50,000), pip values and every prop-firm limit are placeholders. Replace them on the Settings and Lists tabs.

How many trades can it hold?

500 trades on the Journal. Copy the last row's formulas down to extend it, then widen the ranges on the other tabs.

Does the data leave my computer?

No. The workbook runs locally in your spreadsheet app and nothing is sent anywhere. This page only hosts the download.

Is this financial advice?

No. It records and analyses your own trading and is an educational tool. Trading carries risk, including loss of capital.
